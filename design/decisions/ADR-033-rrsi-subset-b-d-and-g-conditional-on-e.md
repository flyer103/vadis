## Background

**The survey proposed an evaluation; the evaluation produced a subset; the owner opened step 1.** The
2026-09-24 instruction was to evaluate RRSI (arXiv:2609.24972v1) for this router. The survey
(`autowork/survey/2026-09-24_rrsi-2609.24972.md` §7) fixed the sequence: step 0 is a **`$0` retrospective
falsification** against this repository's own committed bytes, and step 1 — *"only if step 0 catches ≥ 1
defect honestly"* — is a freeze card that writes the contract for the adopted subset. R36-1 ran step 0 as a
**28-cell** table (`failure × mechanism`, machine-readable in `cells.json`, regenerated byte-identically by
`make_cells.py`), against this repository's **four already-measured failures**:

| id | the failure, in R36-1's own words | anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** | the frozen corpus cannot fire the lever it exists to measure (36 payload nodes, 0 selected, 0 edited) | `491fce2` |
| **F2** | the same flat curve gave two verdicts, and two bases publish two bands | `2d68ddf` |
| **F3** | machinery that cannot earn its place: a live-unreachable rule; a never-wired switch | `9cb88a4` / `8728f0d`; `2777f86` |
| **F4** | a lever published in the direction opposite to its own stated mechanism | `9275bb0` |

The verdict was **6 yes / 22 no / 0 cannot-tell**: **B** (evidence-aware credit) and **D** (leakage
screening) catch two failures each; **G** (structural pruning) catches F3 because the gain is *zero by
construction* and **mis-fires** on F1 and F4; **E** (the noise floor) catches F2 and is the paper's own
first non-compensatory criterion, but its whole content is the band this repository has never calibrated, so
it is the owner's; and **A**, **C**, **F** catch none of the four. The owner's answer — **〈开〉**, open
step 1 — is the decision this ADR records: **the adopted subset enters, and it enters as refusal conditions
with a named artifact each.**

**Why the loop may write these three and not the fourth.** B, D and G are **loop-side**: B is a ledger and
a proposer-side condition over rounds; D's screen is a card-body requirement and its product-side invariant
**already landed** (`crates/router-plugins/src/transform_rules.rs:890` at HEAD, `:880` as landed at
`a429b9a`); G's report is a per-round findings row and its deletion step is ordinary product work through
the four gates. None of the three mints a number: B's parameters are **none** (`accepted` is already
defined by the gate table), D's are **none** (a witness is not a threshold), and G's are **`w`** — a declared
window, the loop's own, like A's `T` — and **`δ`**, which is E's parameter and therefore the owner's
(`autowork/STATE.md:892`; `AGENTS.md` 9). E itself is `needs-human`: choosing a reference basis is a change
to **what a measurement means**, and R33-3-F1/F3 measured what that costs (two bases published two bands;
one flat curve gave two verdicts). So this ADR **registers** E and writes no part of it.

**What the cells fix about the clause shape, and why the shape is not free.** Three of R36-1's six `yes`
cells carry a caveat that a contract must respect, or the contract is decoration:

1. **B/F1's catch rests on a declaration, not a candidate.** The entry that would have caught F1 is the
   evolve set's own machine-readable `exclusion_note` (`autowork/corpus/codex-pair-2026-09-22/manifest.toml:40`),
   which said at the freeze that *"this corpus does not exercise the large tool-output compression target the
   auto tier is for"*. A ledger limited to candidate rows would not contain it. Therefore the ledger ingests
   **the evolve set's own declarations** (and the label ladder's reason fields) as first-class rows.
2. **B/F4's catch is a juxtaposition inside one row.** What catches F4 is a row that carries the hypothesis
   and the `Δcost` **together**, where they contradict in sign (`autowork/harness/cost_model.py:406-407`,
   *"model choice — route work that tolerates it to the cheaper model"*, beside
   `autowork/harness/r34-2/rank.py:120,122`'s `alt_price=PRO` → **+$0.872370**). The ledger therefore
   *juxtaposes* the two fields; it does not test the sign, and no clause here pretends it does.
3. **D's catch is a witness requirement, not an always-refuse rule** — and it is **passable** by a live
   rule, which the evaluation measured, not assumed: at `97ba5b0` `bash-log-noise` carries
   `match_tool = '^(Bash|bash|shell|run_terminal_cmd|exec_command)$'` with **no** `match_kind`
   (`rules/tool_output.toml:183` at this HEAD), and at `a429b9a` `tool-result-json` carries `order = -1`
   (`:124`) with `match_kind = ["log", "text"]` (`:128`) — a kind the declared table does produce. A
   contract that said "refuse anything that adds machinery" would have refused the two repairs this
   repository actually needed.

And one measured fact governs G's form: **the unconditioned rule mis-fires.** Read as written, G reports
`bash-log-noise` for deletion on F1 (its frozen-corpus gain is 0 because the evolve set cannot fire any
rule — the engine stops at `bash-log-noise` on all 36 nodes) and the model-choice lever for deletion on F4
(a capable lever whose **price row** is wrong, not its capability). It would have deleted the lever R35
repaired.

## Decision

### D1 — B (evidence-aware credit assignment) enters as **the candidate ledger plus a pre-spend refusal clause**

**The artifact.** A new committed, loop-side directory **`autowork/ledger/`**, whose contract is its own
`README.md` (this ADR's §Decision D5 gives the reason for the home). The README fixes:

- **the row**, one JSON object per line in `autowork/ledger/candidates.jsonl`, with the fields the
  evaluation names — *the component modified · the hypothesis tested · the source diff · the score change ·
  the cost change · accepted* — spelled as a closed field list with the null-vs-absent rule (`null` **with a
  reason**, never `0`; an absent measurement is `not_run`, never a zero that reads like a measurement —
  ADR-031 D4.5's own rule);
- **three row kinds**, because the entries that catch F1 and F3 are not candidate rows:
  `candidate` · `evolve-declaration` (the evolve set's own machine-readable fields, e.g. the manifest's
  `exclusion_note`) · `label-reason` (the label ladder's reason fields);
- **the `Δcost` rule**: `Δcost` is taken from **`autowork/harness/cost_model.py`** (ADR-031) and the row
  quotes its base, its basis, its `n`, the rate it used and its label; a `Δcost` restated or re-derived
  anywhere else is not a ledger row. This is a pointer, not a second implementation: AGENTS 4's label
  discipline and ADR-031 D2's named-base rule already govern the field;
- **the refusal clause, in the spelling a card can obey** (D1's operative sentence, and the one
  `autowork/work-mode.md` carries into every card body): **before a round's first spend its card names the
  ledger rows whose hypotheses it re-tests; if a named row records that hypothesis as already evaluated,
  the round must either cite the new evidence that distinguishes its test, or refuse and re-scope — and the
  refusal names the row and its `anchor_commit`.**
- **the findings-format addition**: a round's findings section gains, per finding, the triple
  **hypothesis · Δscore · Δcost** where the round measured them, each with its label. `autowork/STATE.md`'s
  waiting-on-human table **keeps** its `class · owner · due round` shape — it is a decision register, not a
  measurement table, and this ADR does not widen it.

**What B is not, and what it owes.** B is not in force until its own falsifying experiment returns.
R36-1 §6.1(d) already defined it and this ADR assigns it: **the card that implements B must build the
ledger for R33–R35 from committed bytes only** (cards, round records, findings ledgers, `STATE.md`
paragraphs) and check that it contains, *before R35's first spend*, a row keyed to R35's premise — *"the
frozen corpus can exercise L2"* — whose recorded outcome contradicts it. **Falsified if that row cannot be
built from committed artifacts** (the fields live only in prose); the honest conclusion would then be that B
is a prose practice, not a ledger, and the adoption is not worth its bookkeeping. That conclusion, if it
comes, is a **new ADR** — this one is append-only and is not rewritten by its own experiment.

### D2 — D (leakage / reachability screen) enters as **a witness requirement at the card level**

**The witness format, fixed so two cards cannot mean two things:**

| what is added | the witness | read where |
|---|---|---|
| a **rule** | a **(tool name, kind)** pair for which `kinds_for_tool(tool)` returns the rule's declared kind, **plus** the locator of the declaration that makes it so | `crates/router-core/src/transform.rs:157` (`TOOL_KINDS`) and `:169` (`kinds_for_tool`) at this HEAD; the table's own test at `:583` |
| a **stage**, a **module** or a **switch** | the **call site's `file:line`** | the tree of the candidate, quoted with the commit that was read |

**The mandatory section and the check point.** A card whose scope adds a rule, a stage, a module or a
switch carries a section **`## Reachability witness`** with five lines: what is added · its kind · the
witness · the anchor (commit + the command that read it) · **what the witness does not establish**. The
check point is the **pre-spend** point, in the same place `writes:` is already checked: the card body carries
it as it is authored — before the round's dependency DAG is drawn and before the card's first commit — and
the reviewer re-reads it against the card's own tip at the close-out. Two forms of the same check, and both
are needed: for a **candidate**, the witness is read at the candidate's **own tree** (that is the
pre-scoring critic the paper describes, and it is what makes an unreachable addition unable to pass); for a
**retrospective** reading (how D's two `yes` cells are decided) it is read at the **failure's anchor**
(`9cb88a4`/`8728f0d`, `2777f86`).

**Both forms of the screen's force, with the owner's half left to the owner.**

1. **Card-level (this is the contract).** A candidate that adds a rule, a stage, a module or a switch is
   **not accepted without a published witness**: absent witness ⇒ refusal, recorded with the diff. The
   round's record may not list such an addition as landed-with-witness, and the reviewer's verdict refuses
   it as an unsupported clause. **This form changes no gate** — it is a card-body obligation and a review
   criterion, exactly like `writes:` — and so it is writable today.
2. **Gate-level (open, and the owner's).** Whether the screen may **block a round** is a precondition on the
   gate table (`autowork/program.md:34-40`) and therefore a human decision (`AGENTS.md` 9; the evaluation
   registered it as owner decision 2). **This contract assumes form 1 only**, and says so here rather than
   letting the choice be implicit.

**What this screen is not.** It is not an always-refuse rule (see the passable witnesses in the
background), and it does **not** re-do the leakage limb: *"the measurement is not part of the search space"*
is already this repository's rule for **who may edit** (`AGENTS.md` 9 / ADR-012 item 2); the gap the survey
named is that it was never a critic on **what a diff adds**, and only the inert-machinery half is new here.

### D3 — G (structural pruning) enters **conditioned on E**, as a per-round report whose verdict vocabulary is fixed

**The artifact.** A per-round **unproductive-components report** built from committed traces, in the shape
R34-2 already uses (`autowork/harness/r34-2/`: a script that reads committed aggregates only, a
machine-readable table, a prose table, and a findings row), landing as a **findings row** in the round
record. The deletion it proposes is ordinary product work through the four gates.

**The conditioned rule, and the vocabulary that makes it obeyable.**

- A component may be reported as a **`deletion-target`** only when **(i)** D's witness says it is
  **live-reachable** and **(ii)** every measurement in the window is either **exactly zero by
  construction** (a rule no tool name can select fires nothing; a switch with no call site runs nothing —
  F3's shape) or **outside E's band `δ`**.
- Where a measurement is **absent**, **inside the band**, or **not attributable from committed bytes**, the
  report's cell reads **`unmeasured`** — the words `non-earning` and *"no strictly positive measured
  gain"* are **not verdicts** in this report; they are the unconditioned rule's input, and the
  unconditioned rule is refused by measurement (it would have reported `bash-log-noise` on F1 and the
  model-choice lever on F4).
- A third cell, `earning`, is available when at least one window measurement is outside the band with the
  sign of a gain.

**Until row 1 is answered, every cell is `unmeasured`.** The report may still be produced — its D-witness
column and its zero-by-construction zeros are decidable today — but **no deletion target may be reported**
while `δ` does not exist: that is what "conditional on E" means in operational terms, and it is why adopting
the conditioned form costs the loop almost nothing today and claims almost nothing either.

**The parameters and their owners.** The pruning window **`w`** is the **loop's** — a declaration, like A's
`T` — and it is declared **inside the report**; this ADR and `autowork/work-mode.md` write **no value** for
it (a number in a contract file is a second copy that goes stale, and a default would be a threshold by
another name). The band **`δ`** is the **owner's**: it is E's parameter, and every use of it inside a verdict
is `autowork/STATE.md:892`'s row-1 question.

**The attribution limit is a clause, not a footnote.** `R33-4-F1` records that raw traces are gitignored
(`.gitignore:29`'s `state/` class), so a window's measurements are **recomputable only in the tree that
produced them**. The report therefore carries an explicit **`not_attributable`** list: for every component
whose window it cannot re-derive from committed bytes, it says so, and that component's cell is
`unmeasured`. A window that cannot be re-derived is never a zero.

### D4 — E (the noise band) is **registered, not written**

The decision, as one line the owner can answer (verbatim from R36-1 §6.4):

> **Does this repository's base apparatus get a measured noise band — i.e. a named reference basis (all
> records, warm-up excluded, or another) over which the unchanged base is evaluated repeatedly, and does that
> band enter any round verdict — or does the loop keep citing *declared + measured* and publish no band?**

**Files that force it:** `autowork/STATE.md:892` (waiting-on-human **row 1**; state cell *"**open** — the
loop writes declared+measured, invents no number"*), **`AGENTS.md` 9** (`AGENTS.md:41`), and the measured
evidence at `autowork/STATE.md:676` (`R33-3-F1` two bases, two bands; `R33-3-F3` two verdicts on one flat
curve). The loop-side half that could be adopted without the owner — *evaluate the base repeatedly and
publish the spread* — is refused here for a reason the cells supply: publishing a band **without naming its
basis** publishes a second undeclared reference, i.e. reproduces F2 exactly. So E stays the owner's, and
nothing downstream of it is measured honestly before it is answered.

## Rationale

- **Three clauses, three artifacts, one traceable cell each.** Every clause above reduces to a cell:
  the ledger's declaration rows ← **B/F1 (`yes`)**; the row's hypothesis-beside-`Δcost` shape ← **B/F4
  (`yes`)**; the witness requirement ← **D/F3 (`yes`)** and **D/F1-limb1 (`yes`)**; the conditioned verdict
  vocabulary ← **G/F3 (`yes`)** plus **G/F1 and G/F4 (the two `no`s that are mis-fires)**; the E-condition ←
  **E/F2 (the table's only `yes` for E)** and E's `needs-human` class. `autowork/harness/r37-1/clauses.json`
  is the machine-readable form of that mapping, and `make_clauses.py` regenerates it: **17 clauses, 42
  references, all 28 cells (6 `yes` / 22 `no`), every reference verified against the committed `cells.json`
  including its verdict** — so a clause whose cell stopped resolving fails the script instead of surviving as
  prose. Clause `C-1` writes the six boundary cells (D/F2, D/F4, E/F1, E/F3, E/F4, G/F2) explicitly, so no
  clause above can be read as broader than its cells.
- **Nothing enters the measurement.** No clause touches the gate table, the corpus, the conformance
  assertions, the L1 envelope, the replay code or any threshold. The three obligations bind **loop-side
  artifacts** — a ledger, a card section, a findings row — which is the half of RRSI that R36-1's §7 table
  says can be measured honestly today.
- **The honest cost is stated.** B's bookkeeping is not free (a row per candidate and per declaration), and
  its own experiment may falsify it. G's report is cheap to produce and says almost nothing until E — that is
  exactly what conditioning on E means, and it is better than a report that says `non-earning` on a
  measurement that does not exist.
- **The rule is enforceable without discipline.** A card with no `## Reachability witness` is refused by a
  reviewer reading a card; a round whose card re-tests an evaluated hypothesis without new evidence is
  refused before it spends; G's vocabulary makes `non-earning` an unwritable word for a measurement that was
  not taken. Each is a criterion a second reader can apply mechanically.

## Consequences

- `autowork/ledger/` exists as a committed directory with its contract and **no data file**: the ledger's
  `candidates.jsonl` is created by the card that implements B, together with the falsifying experiment. An
  empty or invented ledger row would be evidence for nothing.
- `autowork/work-mode.md` gains one section; `design/DESIGN.md` gains §12.19; both are loop/design-side and
  neither changes what a gate means.
- The three obligations apply **from the next round's freeze card onward** — including to the round that
  implements them.
- **One obligation is owed and registered, not written** (this card's write set excludes `program.md`):
  `program.md`'s step 1 (direction → card) and step 4.5 (findings triage) should carry one line pointing at
  `work-mode.md`'s new section, so a reader of the charter alone learns the card owes the declarations.
  Owner: **the orchestrator**, due **R37**.
- **If B's experiment fails**, the honest outcome is a new ADR recording that B is a prose practice here;
  this ADR is not rewritten. **If G is ever to report a deletion target**, it needs E answered first; until
  then the report exists and says `unmeasured`.
- The **selection side of RRSI stays blocked on the corpus question** exactly as R36-1 §5 item 3 measured:
  every selection-side parameter is calibrated on an evolve environment, and this repository's corpus cannot
  carry a `verified` figure (`D3` unmet since the ladder existed). Nothing in this ADR moves that, and
  nothing here may be reported as a saving.

## What this ADR does not decide

- **E's reference basis, its band, and whether a band enters any verdict** (row 1; `AGENTS.md:41`).
- **Whether D's screen may block a round** (a gate-table precondition; owner decision 2 of R36-1 §8).
- **Any cost-acceptance envelope** (F's `β0`/`β1`) — registered only in the conditional form the survey §6
  names: adopting it would mint a **second** envelope beside the still-unwritten latency one.
- **The signed corpus and its promotion** (`R35-1-F2`, the FREEZE DISCLOSURE; ADR-012 item 2). It remains
  the human's act and, separately, the orchestrator's.
- **Any value for `w`**, any default for it, and any per-component budget.
- **Any product-side change of any kind**, including the wording decision on `TOOL_KINDS`
  (`R35-1-F1`, owner `human`).
